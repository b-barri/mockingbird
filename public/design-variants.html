How to choose

The two decisions are independent — you can pick any input-toggle variant with any feedback variant. But there's a vibe pairing worth considering: V3 + Variant C feels like a structured "interview platform" identity; V1 + Variant A feels like a coach-meets-tool identity; V2 + Variant B is the middle of both axes.

Once you've picked, tell me toggle: V<n>, feedback: Variant <A|B|C> and I'll implement it. If Variant B or C is chosen, the requirements doc gets an amendment first (small but real — should be visible in PR history that the scope decision was reversed deliberately).
