Mockingbird · product prep redesign

Three visual directions for the two-screen onboarding flow (API key entry → case type selection). Same copy, same components, same design tokens — three different opinions on what kind of product Mockingbird is. Click a card to view at full fidelity; each variant cross-links to the other two.

A.
Direction A

Editorial Studio

Magazine-style two-column layout, large Instrument Serif headlines, and case types presented as a contents page with serif Roman numerals in coral. The mascot becomes an editorial illustration credit. Reads like the front matter of a thoughtful magazine.

  • Two-column Step 1 with pull-quote notes
  • Hairline-underline inputs (no boxed fields)
  • I. / II. / III. / IV. case list

Direction B

Pre-flight Console

JetBrains Mono is the primary chrome. ASCII section dividers, ●READY / ○PENDING status dots, a meter-style budget bar, and a 2×2 grid of “draft cards” with stat blocks (DIFFICULTY, BANK, TIME, STATUS). Engineered, deliberate, slightly nerdy in a craftsman way.

  • Mono labels and ASCII dividers
  • Tan-bg config panels with crisp borders
  • Draft cards with mono stat blocks

Direction C

Backstage Quiet

Subtractive. Single-column on each side. Hairline fields, dramatic vertical breathing room, one focal case rendered as editorial type (no card chrome), V2 cases demoted to one footer line of italic text. Coral appears only on the two primary CTAs. The mascot sits small at the foot, like Alex waiting patiently.

  • Single-column on both screens
  • Coral used only on the two CTAs
  • Mascot in sitting/waiting pose, small
